Submission notes for this visualization

Encoding, interaction and value added

Encoding (what each mark means). One main view and four linked sub-views, read through two lenses: buyer and investor.

  • Main view, the price skyline. A tilted map of Dubai. Each community has one tower at its location. Metro lines are drawn on the ground.
  • Buyer lens. Tower height = median price of the chosen home (number of bedrooms). Green = the part within the budget. Orange = the part above it. A fully green tower is within budget.
  • Investor lens. Tower height = median price per m² at the end of the period. Black line = the price at the start. Colour = growth per year compared with the city (red = faster, blue = slower, grey = about the same). Coloured outline = on the shortlist.
  • Other layouts. Flat map (colour only) and cartogram (circle area = number of sales).
  • Sub-view 1, ranked list (both lenses). Bar length = the measure of the lens, sorted.
  • Sub-view 2, community profile (both lenses). Line = median price per m² per quarter, band = middle half of sales, grey line = the city. Buyer lens adds the price ladder: one bar per bedroom count, bar = middle half of prices, tick = median, dashed line = budget, text = median size.
  • Sub-view 3, comet chart (investor). Tail = start of the period, head = end, x = median price per m², y = sales per year (both log), colour = growth compared with the city.
  • Sub-view 4, price index and liquidity (investor). One line per shortlisted community, start = 100; bars = sales per quarter on one common scale.

Interaction.

  • Switch lens (floating bar): the towers sink and grow again with the new meaning, and the sub-views change.
  • Budget slider: the towers change colour while you drag.
  • Period brush: sets the months used. Growth, comets and the index are calculated again and animate.
  • Click a tower, comet, bar or row: it is selected in every sub-view. In the investor lens it is also added to the shortlist (up to three).
  • Hover a row of the ranked list: that community is highlighted on the skyline.
  • Filters: property type, status (existing or off-plan), minimum sales, remove errors. Layout switch, pan and zoom.

Value added. One skyline answers two different questions about price.

  • Buyer: Where can I afford the home I want? How much over budget is it elsewhere? What does each home size cost in one community, and how big is it?
  • Investor: Where is the price still low but rising faster than the city? Did the number of sales grow too (comets)? Does my shortlist still look good when indexed to 100 and checked for enough sales?

Data. Main file: transactions.csv from the Dubai Land Department. It is a random 10% sample, and we do not scale the counts up. We added two files from the course repository:

  • dubai.geojson: community borders, area and population. Linked by community number (comm_num = COMM_NUM).
  • dubai_metro_stations.geojson: the 56 stations. Linked by location: we measure the straight-line distance from each area centre (area_lat, area_lon) to every station and keep the nearest.

All three files are loaded from the course repository each time the page opens.
