Submission notes for this visualization

Encoding, interaction and value added

Encoding (what each mark means). One main view and four linked sub-views, read through two lenses: developer and planner.

  • Main view, growth rings. Drawn like the rings of a tree. Each ring is one year (2015 in the middle, 2026 outside). Each spoke is one community. Each circle is one community in one year, and its area = the number of sales in that year.
  • Developer lens. Spokes are ordered clockwise by the time of the community's off-plan wave. Colour (light to dark orange) = share of off-plan sales in that year. Communities that launched late are empty in the inner rings and dark in the outer ones.
  • Planner lens. The same circles; spokes are ordered clockwise by distance to the nearest metro station. Colour (dark to light blue) = distance band; dark = close. An outer band repeats the distance band.
  • Unrolled layout. The same circles on a straight grid: one row per community, one column per year.
  • Sub-view 1, ranked list (both lenses). Bar length = off-plan sales (developer) or sales of the busiest communities more than 4 km from a station (planner).
  • Sub-view 2, selected community (both lenses). Its spoke as a bar chart: x = year, bar height = sales, orange = off-plan, blue = existing homes.
  • Sub-view 3, launch waves (developer). One row per master development, x = quarter, orange area = off-plan sales, blue line = existing-home sales, rows ordered by the time of the off-plan wave, shaded band = selected period.
  • Sub-view 4, distance bands (planner). x = year, height = share of sales, colour = distance band (always adds to 100%).

Interaction.

  • Switch lens (floating bar): the spokes are sorted again and every circle travels around the rings to its new place.
  • Switch layout: the rings unroll into the grid, and roll up again.
  • Play: a 12-month window moves from 2015 to 2026, ring by ring. The shaded band also crosses the launch waves.
  • Click a circle, a list row or a wave: that community is selected in every sub-view, and its spoke is marked. A click on a wave also brings the rings back on screen.
  • Click a distance band: the page goes up to the rings and the spokes of that band stay highlighted.
  • Hover any mark for its exact numbers.
  • Filters: period brush, property type, status, minimum sales, remove errors. Colour menu.

Value added. One picture shows twelve years of every community at once, with no map.

  • Developer: Where is the launch wave now, and where was it before? Which communities launched together? How long did earlier waves last, and what followed them?
  • Planner: Is the new activity near the metro? No: sales within 2 km of a station fell from 36% (2015) to 24% (2026), and sales more than 8 km away rose from 25% to 29%. The outer rings are heavy on the far side.

Data. Main file: transactions.csv from the Dubai Land Department. It is a random 10% sample, and we do not scale the counts up. We added two files from the course repository:

  • dubai.geojson: the list of communities with their names. Linked by community number (comm_num = COMM_NUM).
  • dubai_metro_stations.geojson: the 56 stations. Linked by location: we measure the straight-line distance from each area centre (area_lat, area_lon) to every station and keep the nearest.

All three files are loaded from the course repository each time the page opens.
